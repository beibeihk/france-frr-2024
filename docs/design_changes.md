# Design changes

## 2026-10-03 — refinements after source and independent design audits, before outcomes

No main outcome or regression has yet been inspected. The official original FRR list contains 17,717 codes; the 2025 list is not used to back-date additions. First-year effects through 2025-06 are primary to avoid conflating July 2025 FRR+ intensity changes with the initial designation. The balanced outcome panel is retained through 2026-06, three months before the register reference date, for additional short-run checks. This lag is a conservative convention, not proof of completeness.

For the average effect, baseline 2019-06–2023-05 and post 2024-07–2025-06 each contain a whole number of complete seasonal years; the 2023-06–2024-06 announcement/legislative period is excluded from the primary average contrast and explicitly shown in event studies. The event-study reference is 2023-05, preceding the June 2023 France Ruralités announcement; exact announcement legal chronology remains independently audited. A robustness contrast uses the last complete pre-announcement year 2022-06–2023-05.

The preferred adjacency design uses an outcome-blind maximum-cardinality disjoint bipartite matching with deterministic commune-code tie-breaking. Every commune appears once. Full adjacency edges remain available for transparency. Shared boundaries must exceed 50 metres in the official 2024 5m cartographic product. This topology cutoff is fixed before estimating outcomes. Controls with 30,000+ fixed pre-treatment residents are excluded from main borders. Estimates are local relative designation responses; absent no-spillover assumptions they do not establish national net additional activity.

Administrative A→F events are closure events, not economic cessations. First SIRET entries minus closure events are called a registration–closure balance, since reopenings also affect active stock. Same-day economic-continuity links are separately identified; being ever a successor is not back-dated to birth. Sector counts require historical NAF Rev.2. Unknown employer status and unknown NAF are explicitly retained in quality diagnostics.

## 2026-10-03 — final pre-results policy-timing and clustering refinements

No outcome estimate has been viewed. Independent institutional audit finds FRR+ publication in July 2025 with legal eligibility starting January 2025. Consequently the primary early-effect window is **2024-07–2024-12**, with identical-season baseline **July–December 2019–2022**. The first-year contrast is secondary and excludes eventual FRR+ communes; it addresses a different population. Event studies still show the whole available panel. The initial proposal of a clean July-2024–June-2025 window is superseded, rather than silently retained.

The fixed border graph has communes from the same EPCI and EPCIs in both treated and control roles. Role-specific two-way clustering therefore misses some cross-role dependence. Primary inference uses **EPCI connected components of the border-comparison graph**, allowing common EPCI shocks across either role. This is a dependence grouping, not a reconstructed legal assignment mechanism. Report component sizes, CR1/t intervals and wild restricted cluster bootstrap for the primary constant-coefficient contrast; département graph components and independent-pair inference are alternatives. More aggregated grouping is not guaranteed to increase every standard error. EPCI-level versus département-level legal assignment paths remain separately audited; no sharp RD will be claimed without their replication.

## 2026-10-03 — diagnostics and corrections after observing outcomes

The first average border estimate was positive while the broad and within-department matched estimates were close to zero. These results were observed before the additions below. The initial short monthly pre-test was insufficient: the full 2019–2023 test rejects, and this failure is retained as a substantive identification limit. The analysis status was corrected to expose both tests rather than the favourable short test alone.

Additional diagnostics subtract pair-specific calendar-month averages from 2019–2022, test the same July–December seasons by year, and extrapolate pair-specific linear pre-announcement trends plus calendar offsets. All variants are retained. The full seasonal-adjusted test still rejects; the annual test is weaker. Linear-trend extrapolation is model-dependent and is not a causal repair. The density-median split is exploratory, added after outcomes; no income or population-decline effect is asserted.

Independent audits identified dependence shared through département, EPCI and bassin de vie. Joint typed graphs with all relevant memberships were added as variance sensitivities. Matched pairs originally grouped by département were also reported with joint EPCI–département components because some EPCIs cross département lines. The matching, sample and point estimates remain the outcome-blind initial choices. These groupings are not labelled verified assignment clusters, and sparse/effectively concentrated groups remain a limitation.

Pair totals and absolute changes on each side are descriptive; no unobserved external counterfactual is invented. The observed means increase on both sides. A positive border contrast plus near-zero wider comparisons is not proof of displacement, national net creation, or a national null effect. Administrative retention and closure/reopen indicators are analysed only after dictionary and independent reconstruction checks; retention comparisons condition on policy-dependent entry composition and are descriptive.

The manuscript is explicitly reframed as initial comparative evidence and identification diagnostics. No causal clearance, human author review, native human French edit, public GitHub release or HAL submission is claimed.

Headquarters status and legal-unit age at establishment registration were added after estimation to complete the descriptive dictionary. The age construction uses the official 1900 unknown-date sentinel and separately flags negative ages. Inspection also found 24 records with a non-sentinel legal-unit creation date before 1800; these dates are flagged as unknown for age only. The 1800 threshold is an analyst rule chosen after inspecting the dates, not an official SIRENE sentinel. These entries remain in registration counts, headquarters classification and the original estimations. No age-based causal result is reported.

## 2026-10-04 — assignment reconstruction and threshold diagnostics after the first results

The initial difference comparisons had already been inspected. The research subsequently recovered official Filosofi2020 and census2020 in COG2023, native territorial income medians, the mountain COG2022 archive and its administrative correspondence. Matching the initial list does not identify actual administrative proposals or population inside partially zoned mountain communes.

These are sequential choices after prior DID results, not external preregistration. A/B/D/E failed fixed support rules; no new registration outcome was estimated in those candidates. V had sufficient support but failed selection/continuity and dependence diagnostics. All four bandwidths, 53 clean monthly levels, four same-season checks and the complete covariate family remain available. The V route is causally closed.

The first successful V outcome run and its preceding protocol/files are retained in `bassin_rd_execution_freeze.json`. Later reruns captured the official mass-point warning, added 90 monthly RBC/HC3 numerical identity checks and stored family corrections. These logging/check changes did not alter outcomes, samples, bandwidths, coefficient estimates or interpretation; they are not represented as pre-result decisions. An initial SQL alias parsing failure occurred before any V result was read.

The broad comparison previously grouped all no-EPCI islands under the placeholder ZZZZZZZZZ. The corrected grouping uses 721 actual EPCIs plus three separate commune primitives: 724 groups. The point estimate is unchanged; its standard error changes by approximately 5.4e-8. An independent raw-panel re-estimation reproduced the correction. Matched estimates are unchanged. A generated institutional macro briefly counted 340 basin codes through mixed int/string CSV inference; explicit string typing corrected it to 314, consistent with the unchanged underlying 3,013-commune reconstruction.

The final contribution is national eligibility reconstruction, administrative measurement and identification diagnostics. Closing the causal routes is preserved as a research result. Internal AI reviews are not described as human peer review or native human editing; sole human authorship and responsibility remain explicit. Account creation, public release and HAL submission are separate observed transactions, recorded only if actually completed.
